# What I learned from a e-commerce project

Back in 2024 a e-commerce client came to me because buyers and sellers had no way to talk inside the product. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I added WebSocket messaging with delivery receipts and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: 51% of transactions started with an in-app message after launch; handled 28k messages a day without incident.
