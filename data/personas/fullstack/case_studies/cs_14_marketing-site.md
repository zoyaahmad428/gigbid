# Fast marketing site and CMS for a B2B SaaS company

**Client:** B2B SaaS company  
**When:** 2024-11 to 2024-12  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
The old site was slow, hard to edit and ranked poorly.

## What I did
- Rebuilt the site in Next.js with static generation.
- Connected a headless CMS so the marketing team could publish alone.
- Fixed accessibility and Core Web Vitals issues.

## Outcome
- Raised Lighthouse performance from 40 to 97.
- Grew organic traffic by 39% over three months.

## Stack
Next.js · React · Tailwind CSS · HTML/CSS
