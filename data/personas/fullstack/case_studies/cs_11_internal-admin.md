# Internal admin tool for a hospitality operations team

**Client:** hospitality company  
**When:** 2023-09 to 2023-09  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Operations ran on shared spreadsheets with no access control.

## What I did
- Modelled the workflow in Django with clear states.
- Built list, filter and bulk-edit screens in React.
- Added CSV import and export for the finance team.

## Outcome
- Replaced 10 spreadsheets.
- Cut weekly reconciliation from 5 hours to 1.

## Stack
Django · React · PostgreSQL
