# Real-time messaging feature for a non-profit marketplace

**Client:** non-profit company  
**When:** 2024-06 to 2024-07  
**Engagement:** fixed-price

## Situation
Buyers and sellers had no way to talk inside the product.

## What I did
- Added WebSocket messaging with delivery receipts.
- Stored conversations with moderation hooks.
- Added push and email notifications for missed messages.

## Outcome
- 34% of transactions started with an in-app message after launch.
- Handled 37k messages a day without incident.

## Stack
Node.js · React · TypeScript
