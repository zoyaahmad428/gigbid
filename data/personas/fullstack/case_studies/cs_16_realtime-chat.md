# Real-time messaging feature for a e-commerce marketplace

*E-commerce client · 2024-11 · 12 weeks · hourly*

Buyers and sellers had no way to talk inside the product. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then added WebSocket messaging with delivery receipts. Next I stored conversations with moderation hooks. Finally, I added push and email notifications for missed messages.

The result: I 51% of transactions started with an in-app message after launch, and handled 28k messages a day without incident.

Tools: Node.js, React, TypeScript.
