# What I learned from a fintech project

Back in 2022 a fintech client came to me because bookings came in by phone and email and were often double-booked. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I built a Django backend with availability rules and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: moved 62% of bookings online within two months; brought double bookings to zero over 13 weeks.
