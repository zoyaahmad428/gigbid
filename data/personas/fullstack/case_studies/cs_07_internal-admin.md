# Internal admin tool for a recruiting operations team

*Recruiting client · 2025-05 · 10 weeks · hourly*

Operations ran on shared spreadsheets with no access control. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then modelled the workflow in Django with clear states. Next I built list, filter and bulk-edit screens in React. Finally, I added CSV import and export for the finance team.

The result: I replaced 11 spreadsheets, and cut weekly reconciliation from 8 hours to 2.

Tools: Django, React, PostgreSQL.
