# Weekly demand forecasting for a insurance business

**Client:** insurance company  
**When:** 2024-03 to 2024-05  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Stock planning relied on last year's numbers and missed seasonal swings.

## What I did
- Built features from sales history, promotions and holidays.
- Compared gradient-boosted trees with a seasonal naive baseline.
- Scheduled weekly retraining and published forecasts to a shared sheet.

## Outcome
- Lowered forecast error (MAPE) from 19% to 11%.
- Reduced overstock by about 11%.

## Stack
Python · Machine Learning · scikit-learn
