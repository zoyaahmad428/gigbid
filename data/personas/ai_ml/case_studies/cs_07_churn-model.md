# Churn prediction model for a edtech subscription product

*Edtech client · 2021-08 · 14 weeks · hourly*

The retention team could not tell which accounts were about to leave. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then built account-level features from usage and billing events. Next I trained and calibrated a gradient-boosted classifier. Finally, I explained predictions per account so the team trusted the scores.

The result: I reached ROC-AUC of 0.84, and helped retention calls save about 11% of flagged accounts.

Tools: Python, Machine Learning, scikit-learn.
