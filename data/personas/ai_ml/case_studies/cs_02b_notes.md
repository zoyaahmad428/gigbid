# What I learned from a logistics project

Back in 2023 a logistics client came to me because incoming tickets were routed by hand and often reached the wrong team. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I labelled a sample of historical tickets with the support lead and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: routed 65% of tickets automatically; raised macro-F1 from 0.62 to 0.86.
