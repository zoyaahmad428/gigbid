# Semantic product search for a manufacturing catalogue

**Client:** manufacturing company  
**When:** 2023-09 to 2023-12  
**Engagement:** fixed-price

## Situation
Keyword search failed on synonyms and misspellings, so users left.

## What I did
- Embedded product titles and descriptions.
- Blended semantic and keyword scores and tuned the weights on click logs.
- A/B tested the new ranking against the old one.

## Outcome
- Raised search click-through by 14%.
- Cut zero-result searches by 33%.

## Stack
Python · NLP · Vector Database
