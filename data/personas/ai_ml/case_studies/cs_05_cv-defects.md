# Visual defect detection on a B2B SaaS production line

**Client:** B2B SaaS company  
**When:** 2021-07 to 2021-07  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Quality checks were manual and inconsistent between shifts.

## What I did
- Collected and labelled images from the line cameras.
- Trained an object detection model and tuned it for small defects.
- Exported the model for an edge device next to the line.

## Outcome
- Caught 96% of defects in the hold-out set.
- Ran at 26 frames per second on the edge box.

## Stack
Python · Computer Vision · PyTorch · Deep Learning
