# What I learned from a media project

Back in 2025 a media client came to me because support agents spent too long searching policy PDFs for answers to repeat questions. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I chunked policy PDFs by heading and indexed them in a vector database and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: cut average handling time by 33%; reached 86% answer accuracy on a 200-question test set.
