# Meeting and call summarisation for a B2B SaaS sales team

*B2b saas client · 2023-01 · 13 weeks · fixed-price*

Reps wrote call notes by hand and CRM records were thin. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then transcribed calls and split them by speaker. Next I prompted an LLM to produce structured notes and next steps. Finally, I evaluated summaries against notes written by the sales lead.

The result: I saved reps about 9 minutes per call, and 85% of summaries were accepted without edits.

Tools: Python, LLM, NLP.
