# Sales performance dashboard for a B2B SaaS company

**Client:** B2B SaaS company  
**When:** 2025-06 to 2025-06  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Leadership got sales numbers from three teams that never agreed.

## What I did
- Agreed one definition for each metric with finance.
- Modelled the data in SQL into a clean star schema.
- Built a Power BI report with drill-down by region and rep.

## Outcome
- Replaced 8 manual weekly reports.
- Cut month-end reporting from 6 days to 1.

## Stack
SQL · Power BI · Data Visualization
