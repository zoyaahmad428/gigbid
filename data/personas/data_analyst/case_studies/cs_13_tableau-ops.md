# Operations KPI dashboard in Tableau for a hospitality company

*Hospitality client · 2025-12 · 15 weeks · fixed-price*

Managers could not see delays until customers complained. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then connected Tableau to the operations database. Next I built KPI views for delay, throughput and backlog. Finally, I set threshold alerts for each site.

The result: I cut average delay by 15% after the first quarter, and rolled the dashboard out to 11 sites.

Tools: Tableau, SQL, Data Visualization.
