# Customer survey analysis for a edtech organisation

**Client:** edtech company  
**When:** 2022-08 to 2022-09  
**Engagement:** fixed-price

## Situation
Thousands of survey responses sat unread every quarter.

## What I did
- Cleaned and weighted the responses.
- Coded open-text answers into themes.
- Reported drivers of satisfaction with clear charts.

## Outcome
- Analysed 8680 responses.
- Identified 5 drivers that explained most dissatisfaction.

Statistics · Data Analysis · Excel
