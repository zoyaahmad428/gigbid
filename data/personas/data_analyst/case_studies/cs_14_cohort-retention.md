# Customer cohort and retention analysis for a insurance app

**Client:** insurance company  
**When:** 2024-05 to 2024-08  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Growth looked healthy but revenue was flat.

## What I did
- Built monthly cohorts from event data.
- Measured retention and revenue per cohort.
- Identified the onboarding step where most users dropped.

## Outcome
- Found a 43% drop-off at one onboarding step.
- The fix raised 30-day retention by 6 points.

SQL · Python · Data Analysis
