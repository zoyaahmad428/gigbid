# Self-serve reporting in Looker Studio for a legal services agency

**Client:** legal services company  
**When:** 2025-01 to 2025-03  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
Account managers asked the analyst for the same reports every week.

## What I did
- Built a clean reporting table per client in BigQuery.
- Designed Looker Studio templates per client type.
- Trained account managers to filter and export.

## Outcome
- Cut analyst report requests by 69%.
- Served 24 client accounts from one template.

## Stack
Looker · BigQuery · SQL
