# Customer cohort and retention analysis for a legal services app

*Legal services client · 2023-06 · 8 weeks · hourly*

Growth looked healthy but revenue was flat. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then built monthly cohorts from event data. Next I measured retention and revenue per cohort. Finally, I identified the onboarding step where most users dropped.

The result: I found a 55% drop-off at one onboarding step, and the fix raised 30-day retention by 8 points.

Tools: SQL, Python, Data Analysis.
