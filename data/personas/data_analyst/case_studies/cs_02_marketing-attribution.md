# Marketing channel attribution analysis for a manufacturing brand

**Client:** manufacturing company  
**When:** 2025-10 to 2025-12  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
The team could not say which channels drove sales.

## What I did
- Joined ad platform exports with order data.
- Compared last-click with a position-based model.
- Presented budget shifts with expected impact ranges.

## Outcome
- Found that 27% of spend went to channels with no measurable return.
- The reallocated budget lowered cost per order by 13%.

SQL · Python · Statistics
