# Excel reporting automation for a insurance finance team

*Insurance client · 2022-03 · 12 weeks · fixed-price*

A weekly report took a full day of copy-paste across workbooks. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then rebuilt the report with Power Query and a clean data model. Next I replaced manual lookups with structured references. Finally, I documented the refresh steps for the team.

The result: I cut the weekly report from 6 hours to 1, and removed 8 recurring manual errors found in the old process.

Tools: Excel, Data Analysis.
