# What I learned from a legal services project

Back in 2023 a legal services client came to me because growth looked healthy but revenue was flat. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I built monthly cohorts from event data and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: found a 55% drop-off at one onboarding step; the fix raised 30-day retention by 8 points.
