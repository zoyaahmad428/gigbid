# Data cleaning and deduplication for a fintech CRM

**Client:** fintech company  
**When:** 2026-02 to 2026-04  
**Engagement:** hourly

## Situation
The CRM had duplicate and inconsistent records that broke reporting.

## What I did
- Profiled every field and agreed rules with the client.
- Matched duplicates with fuzzy matching and manual review of edge cases.
- Delivered a clean file and a repeatable script.

## Outcome
- Merged about 9% duplicate records.
- Cleaned 101k records.

## Stack
Python · pandas · Data Analysis
