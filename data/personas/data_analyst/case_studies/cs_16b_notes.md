# What I learned from a recruiting project

Back in 2025 a recruiting client came to me because the team could not say which channels drove sales. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I joined ad platform exports with order data and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: found that 29% of spend went to channels with no measurable return; the reallocated budget lowered cost per order by 9%.
