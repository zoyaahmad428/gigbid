# What I learned from a legal services project

Back in 2025 a legal services client came to me because account managers asked the analyst for the same reports every week. The interesting part was not the model or the code. It was agreeing what to measure. Once we had that, I built a clean reporting table per client in BigQuery and the rest followed.

Numbers, for the record: cut analyst report requests by 69%; served 24 client accounts from one template.
