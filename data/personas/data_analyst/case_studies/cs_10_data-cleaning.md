# Data cleaning and deduplication for a insurance CRM

*Insurance client · 2024-10 · 4 weeks · hourly*

The CRM had duplicate and inconsistent records that broke reporting. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then profiled every field and agreed rules with the client. Next I matched duplicates with fuzzy matching and manual review of edge cases. Finally, I delivered a clean file and a repeatable script.

The result: I merged about 10% duplicate records, and cleaned 85k records.

Tools: Python, pandas, Data Analysis.
