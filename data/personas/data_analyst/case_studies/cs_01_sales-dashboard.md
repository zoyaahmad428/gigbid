# Sales performance dashboard for a non-profit company

*Non-profit client · 2025-07 · 7 weeks · hourly*

Leadership got sales numbers from three teams that never agreed. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then agreed one definition for each metric with finance. Next I modelled the data in SQL into a clean star schema. Finally, I built a Power BI report with drill-down by region and rep.

The result: I replaced 5 manual weekly reports, and cut month-end reporting from 3 days to 2.

Tools: SQL, Power BI, Data Visualization.
