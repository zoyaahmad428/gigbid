# Marketing channel attribution analysis for a recruiting brand

*Recruiting client · 2025-08 · 6 weeks · fixed-price*

The team could not say which channels drove sales. I was brought in to fix that.

I started by working out what good looked like with the client, then joined ad platform exports with order data. Next I compared last-click with a position-based model. Finally, I presented budget shifts with expected impact ranges.

The result: I found that 29% of spend went to channels with no measurable return, and the reallocated budget lowered cost per order by 9%.

Tools: SQL, Python, Statistics.
